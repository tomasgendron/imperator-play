# Imperator

Imperator is a strategy board game set in the Roman world, for 2 or 3 players, played in the browser. Each player is a general:
conquer provinces, make alliances, play cards and build, and be the first to reach 8 Prestige.

## Play
Open **https://tomasgendron.github.io/imperator-play/** in a desktop browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari). Nothing to install.
- **Hot-seat:** 2 or 3 people share one screen and take turns ("Hide hand" covers your cards between turns).
- **Versus AI:** set any seat to AI on the setup screen.
Games autosave in your browser; "Save game" can also export a file you can load later.

If the page looks broken right after an update, reload it with Ctrl+Shift+R (Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac).
